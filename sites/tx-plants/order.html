How it works

No carts, no accounts — just a real grower

Texas Roots is one person and one greenhouse, so ordering is refreshingly human. Here's exactly what happens after you hit send.

1

You tell me what you want

Seeds, rooted cuttings, a starter box, or "help me pick." A rough idea is plenty — we'll sort the details together.

2

I reply within a day

You get a real email back from Jordan with what's available, a total, and a simple payment link. Questions welcome.

3

It ships the same day

Packed by hand with a free grow guide, mailed flat-rate from El Campo. Local? Pick up at the stand and skip shipping.
