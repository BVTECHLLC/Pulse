How we grow

Living soil, not shortcuts.

What's under a plant matters as much as the plant. Here's exactly how the things I sell are grown.

The house blend

Most plants grow in a live-soil house blend — compost, Texas topsoil, and a stack of natural additives over a quality base. Biologically active dirt that feeds the plant the slow, real way.

FoxFarm at the base

For the more expensive plants, I start from a FoxFarm base and build up from there — a richer foundation for the plants that earn it. No cutting corners where it counts.

Hydro when it fits

Some plants I grow hydroponically when that's genuinely the better method — but most of what's destined for your garden is grown in living soil and organic, non-bleached cloth pots.

Want the full breakdown? Read how I test and build soil and why I grow in organic cloth pots in the Almanac.
