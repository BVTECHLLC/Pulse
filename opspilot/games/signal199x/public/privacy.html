Privacy Notice — Operator Review Required

This draft must be reviewed and completed before a public launch. It describes the code in this version, not any other systems a hosting provider may add.

Data collected

Accounts store a username, cryptographic password hash and salt, session token digest, character progress, owned virtual items, quest and housing data, and timestamps. In-game reports store the IDs of the reporting and reported accounts, a selected reason, and time. Explicit block lists are stored per account.

Use and protection

Game data is used for sign-in, saving progress, multiplayer play, abuse prevention and moderation. Session tokens are kept in HttpOnly cookies. Passwords are salted and scrypt-hashed. Normal gameplay does not include trackers, ad networks, payments, or real-world profiles.

Retention and deletion

Permanent character data stays until an operator deletes it. Guests are removed after their session and cleanup, although brief operational records and backups may remain. Operators should define and publish a definite retention period for moderation reports and backups, enable encrypted backups, and provide a verified method for account deletion requests.

Public visibility

Nearby players can see your username, outfit, level and in-game actions. If free-text chat is enabled later, nearby users will see messages. Do not share real-world contact details.

Age limitations

This prototype is intended only for players age 13 and older. It does not include parental consent tooling and should not be opened to children under 13. Depending on jurisdictions and actual audience, additional child-privacy requirements may apply.

Before going live, add the real operator's legal identity, contact address, processing purposes and legal bases, regional rights and disclosures, privacy request instructions, hosting location, and an accurate retention schedule. Obtain legal/privacy review.
