Community Rules

Respect comes first. Signal 199X is a cooperative game, not a place for harassment or unsafe contact.

Before playing

This prototype is for players 13 and older only. Under-13 accounts are not supported. No one can guarantee an online game is completely safe; parents and guardians should review the community rules and supervise where appropriate.

Not allowed

  • Bullying, threats, hate speech, sexual content, or abuse
  • Personal contact information, addresses, phone numbers, social media handles, or off-platform invitations
  • Spam, advertisements, scams, impersonating staff, or attempting to steal accounts
  • Automation, exploiting bugs, cheating, or using the game to attack other players or the hosting infrastructure
  • Sharing other people's personal information or soliciting private messages

Designed for safer play

Free-text chat is disabled by default; approved quick-chat phrases are available instead. PvP requires opt-in and is limited to the Spark Yard. Players can block or report others. Operators may mute, suspend, or remove accounts as needed. Moderation filters may miss unsafe conduct; please report concerns through in-game tools.

For urgent safety issues, contact local emergency services. In-game reports are not emergency services.

These rules are a starting point for an operator to review before public launch.
