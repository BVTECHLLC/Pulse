Turn on "Sign in with Microsoft/Google" on the Pulse login page. Add the redirect URI below to your Microsoft (Entra) and/or Google OAuth app. Microsoft can reuse your M365 mailbox app — just add the redirect URI + delegated openid email profile User.Read permissions to it. In the Entra app, set Supported account types → "Accounts in any organizational directory" so you and every client's M365 org can sign in.
