# Signal 199X — Creative & Gameplay Direction

**Tone:** Nighttime cassette-tape nostalgia with affectionate humor, friendly oddballs, pink-and-mint pixel lighting, an original score, and a world where music is disappearing from memory. This is original intellectual property with nostalgic inspiration, not a recreation or derivative work of EarthBound's world, story or characters.

### Existing play loops

- **Wander:** six contrasting districts with hidden radio towers and treasure, eccentric people, surprise monster types, a seaside road, arcade village, PvP yard and neighborhood homes.
- **Adventure:** make the Lost Mixtape by discovering any three radio towers; story progress is earned from exploration, not repetitive fetch/grind tasks.
- **Team:** direct invite-and-accept parties (up to four players), Heartwave teammate heal, two-person radio broadcast, and three gated dungeon instances with shared bosses and individual loot credit.
- **Skill:** Memory Jam and Pixel Fishing four-color memory challenges; unlock little finds with limited daily currency reward.
- **Cozy:** a personal house with room palette and favorite furnishing, persistent backpack and collectible trinkets, retro chiptune music, quick-chat and emotes.
- **Compete fairly:** only opt-in arena PvP; avoid pay-to-win and gear-dependent combat outcomes.

### Three Rift names

1. **Rainy Radio Tower** — a storm of tangled antennas.
2. **Phantom Arcade** — a dusty game cabinet leaking monsters into the airwaves.
3. **Midnight Moonstation** — a strange signal from beyond the sky.

At this stage the three Rifts are variations of one functional instanced arena layout with different environment colors, spawn strengths and reward scaling, rather than large multi-floor authored dungeons. Bosses are server-side enemy entities. **Rift UI uses world buttons and a teleported isolated map; no matchmaking lobby yet.**

### Extra ideas for the next build

- True staged cooperative boss mechanics (one player jams a transmitter while another defends; coordinated shield windows, revives and phase changes).
- Make houses explorable 2.5D rooms with cosmetic furniture placement and host-controlled visitor permissions.
- Neighborhood block party, DDR-like synchronized rhythm event, seasonal scavenger hunts, and a player-authored mixtape jukebox with only licensed/original music.
- Richer original character sprites (4-direction animation, 3D lighting, character creator cosmetics), higher-resolution tiles, better mobile camera, optional 3D or WebGL post-processing.
- Accessibility: scalable typography, motion reduction, input remapping, high contrast, assistive controls, translation and moderation localized for each supported language.

### Ethical constraints

Free-to-play without real-money advantages, loot boxes for cash, gambling or trading. Reward diverse forms of play. Anti-harassment defaults (controlled chat and voluntary arena) before social scale. Avoid copying Nintendo/HAL art, places, dialogue and music. The original creative expression must remain distinct.
