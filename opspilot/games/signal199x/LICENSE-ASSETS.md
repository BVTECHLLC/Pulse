# ART, AUDIO AND THIRD-PARTY LICENSE NOTES

## Included in v3 (self-hosted)

- `public/engine3d.js`: original procedural low-poly environment, avatars, creatures and objects authored for SIGNAL 199X. No extracted/recreated Pokémon, EarthBound, Warcraft, or other proprietary models/textures.
- `public/media/signal-world-cover.webp`: original generated illustration used as a title-screen backdrop. **Illustrative cover concept art, not an in-game render or representation of actual renderer quality.**
- Browser audio: original Web Audio oscillator/chiptune melody and original synthesized button/combat feedback; generated in-browser with no third-party audio file. No CDN connection required.

## Free third-party assets researched but **NOT downloaded / included**

The build environment could not retrieve remote asset ZIP archives. The following third-party libraries have per-pack CC0 licenses verified on official pages, and are safe places to source **optional future replacements** only after checking licenses from the downloaded archives:

- https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit — 330 CC0 low-poly nature props.
- https://kenney.nl/assets/retro-fantasy-kit — check the official page and license when adding.
- https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds — 100 CC0 UI sounds.
- https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds — CC0 impacts.
- https://kenney.nl/support — Kenney's licensing FAQ (commercial usage allowed, attribution not required for their asset packs).

Do not claim these packs were imported in the current build. If you choose to add them later, retain their archive license, file hashes and original source URLs. Avoid downloading and republishing Nintendo/Blizzard sprites, music, maps, character likenesses or ripped client assets.
