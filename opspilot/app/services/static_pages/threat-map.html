6 moves that stop most of this yourself

  • ✓Turn on MFA everywhere — email, banking, remote access. It blocks the overwhelming majority of account takeovers.
  • ✓Use a password manager — unique password per site, so one leak can't unlock everything.
  • ✓Patch within days — turn on auto-updates for your OS, browser, router, and apps.
  • ✓Keep one offline backup — updated, then unplugged. Ransomware can't reach what isn't connected.
  • ✓Agree a code word — for your family and finance team, to defeat deepfake "it's me, send money" calls.
  • ✓Slow down on links — AI writes flawless phishing now; verify unexpected requests out of band.
