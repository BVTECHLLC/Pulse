How a Texas Roots order works.

The whole thing runs out of one house. That's the point — low overhead means honest prices and plants that were alive an hour before they were boxed.

01

You pick

Choose seeds, cuttings, or a starter box and send your order through the form.

02

We propagate

Seeds get counted and carded; cuttings get taken, rooted, and checked before shipping.

03

We pack

Damp-wrapped and bubble-mailed by hand, with a printed grow guide in every package.

04

You grow

It lands in your mailbox in a few days. Scan the guide, pot it up, and you're off.

Who's behind it

Started by Jordan Polasek, one garden at a time.

Texas Roots was founded by Jordan Polasek, an El Campo technologist who got tired of mail-order plants showing up half-dead in a box. So he started sending his own — from his own greenhouse, propagated by hand, with his goldendoodle Autumn supervising.

Jordan also runs JordanPolasek.com and built this entire storefront himself. The same person who tends the beds writes the code and packs the boxes. Order from Texas Roots and you're ordering straight from Jordan Polasek's garden bench in El Campo.
