# Signal 199X Balance Notes

Goal: gear collection is exciting but not a prerequisite for enjoying the game. Relaxed explorers can complete the first story by tuning **any 3/6 radio towers** without killing an enemy. Co-op Rifts scale enemy HP upward with party size (1 + .45 per additional player) while each participating teammate earns individual XP/coin/loot rolls. There are six regions, 10 one-time chests, over 20 loot items, 5 rarity tiers and a communal boss.

## Loot odds

Ordinary enemy defeat: **29% chance of an item** per eligible contributor. Conditional item rarity weights: **common 68%, uncommon 21%, rare 8%, epic 2.6%, legendary 0.4%**. Effective legendary item odds from a normal eligible kill: about **0.116%** (roughly 1 in 862) before factors like kill speed or slot selection. This is intentionally rare but not a progression requirement.

Open-world Hushling boss: guaranteed loot with **rare 82%, epic 16%, legendary 2%**. Instanced Rift boss: **rare 82%, epic 16%, legendary 2%** from normal kill credit; completing a Rift provides a separate team bonus item weighted **uncommon 40%, rare 48%, epic 11.5%, legendary 0.5%** and gold/XP. Rifts are replayable.

## Economy

- Start with 75 stardust, a stick and 3 healing drinks.
- Lost Mixtape: each first-time signal yields 12 coins +32 XP; completing three yields 140 additional coins +160 XP + Rabbit-Ear Antenna.
- Persistent housing costs **450** in-game stardust, furniture or wallpaper change **35**.
- Memory Jam / Pixel Fishing: a successful pattern returns **10–23** stardust, with a strict **12 rewarded successes per UTC day per account**. Repeats still playable once the cap is reached after the next UTC day; no commercial currency is involved.
- Duo Broadcast: 2 distinct nearby players must activate the same beacon within 20 seconds, gaining 35 coins and 45 XP per participant; 90-second group cooldown.
- PvP is opt-in, limited to Spark Yard and **never drops money or gear**. PvP damage is computed as a percentage of the opponent's total HP, independently of equipped attack/defense. Repeating victories against the same opponent within five minutes do not increase wins.
- No player trading or cash-out is available; this helps prevent bot-driven gold transfer, money-laundering abuse and marketplace scams during alpha.

These are **initial tuning targets, not a claim of perfectly balanced gameplay**. Monitor the distribution of drops, Rift clear rates, level/gear discrepancy, player retention, moderation volume and play styles in a supervised alpha. Rare items can be powerful in PvE without determining PvP outcomes.
