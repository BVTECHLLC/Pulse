The attacks hitting small businesses this year aren't sophisticated. They're just aimed at the gaps most owners don't know they have.

Three we're seeing every week across Houston, Sugar Land, Austin and San Antonio:

→ AI-written invoices from "your vendor" with the bank details quietly swapped.
→ Login prompts spammed to your phone at 2am until someone taps "approve."
→ A VPN or firewall nobody has patched in a year, sitting wide open to the internet.

None of these need a bigger budget to stop. They need the basics done deliberately: verify payment changes by phone, switch off tap-to-approve MFA, and give every internet-facing device an owner and a patch schedule.

That's most of the job. The rest is monitoring it so a small gap never becomes a bad week.
